## Writing Desk Integration

When applying a stylized aesthetic from this vault:

1. Identify the intended visual and tonal palette.
2. Collect key motifs, symbols, and recurring imagery.
3. Define the lexical style (diction, rhythm, texture).
4. Verify consistency across adjacent scenes.
5. Ensure style serves clarity and story momentum.

## Decision Framework

### Core Priorities (In Order)

1. Cyber-Duat fusion > generic cyberpunk
2. Psychological claustrophobia > action spectacle
3. Dry British satire > melodrama
4. Ancient Egyptian weight > sparkly fantasy

### Issue Triage

| Severity | Description |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | High fantasy tropes (wands, sparkles, mana); Voice drift into generic noir or generic epic fantasy; Missing Cyber-Duat fusion (tech + myth not blended) |
| HIGH | Over-explained magic or tech (no mystery); No British understatement or dry wit; Flat emotional interiority |
| MEDIUM | More sensory blending needed; Stronger Egyptian texture needed; Sharper socio-political bite needed |

## Analysis Framework

### Core Atmosphere

- Techno-Dystopia weight (invasive tech)
- Hidden Heritage (Egyptian myth in plain sight)
- Psychological claustrophobia (internal state focus)

### Voice Satire

- British understatement
- Dry wit under pressure
- Political and class critique

### Fusion Rules

- Tech plus myth blended, not separated
- Gritty realism over sparkle fantasy
