# Critique Framework

Use this rubric with zero leniency. Findings must be evidence-backed and operational.

## Scoring
- Severity:
  - `P0`: Breaks trust, logic, chronology, or core dramatic engine.
  - `P1`: Major quality drag that weakens tension or clarity.
  - `P2`: Noticeable weakness but not fatal.
  - `P3`: Polish-level issue.
- Confidence: `High`, `Medium`, `Low`.

## 1) Core Themes and Tension
### Dual Roles (Celebrity vs Operative)
- Check whether role conflict creates active decisions, not only reflection.
- Failure pattern: protagonist observes pressure without counter-move.

### Identity Shift (Glamour -> Secrecy)
- Check whether transition is causally earned by escalating risk.
- Failure pattern: abrupt mode switch without operational triggers.

### Lockdown Impact (Ch5 and Ch9 emphasis)
- Check whether confinement changes tactics, voice, and self-image.
- Failure pattern: confinement becomes static waiting.

### Agency Under Surveillance
- Check whether surveillance pressure produces strategic action.
- Failure pattern: repeated intake of threats with no command response.

## 2) Narrative Balance and Structure
### Struggle vs Strategy
- Check smooth handoff from identity struggle into custody/legal war.
- Failure pattern: phase shift feels like a different book.

### Emotion vs OpSec
- Check emotional damage is present while procedures stay readable.
- Failure pattern: protocol density drowns emotional stakes.

### Technical Integration
- Check continuity protocols are understandable to non-specialists.
- Failure pattern: unexplained jargon walls.

### Pacing and Time Leaps
- Check transitions preserve causality and pressure.
- Failure pattern: unexplained jumps or backward chronology.

## 3) Setting, Atmosphere, and Context
### Cairo as Plot Engine
- Check unrest actively changes routes, legal windows, and surveillance behavior.
- Failure pattern: unrest functions only as backdrop flavor.

### Sensory Immersion
- Check heat, dust, noise, and crowd physics raise scene stress.
- Failure pattern: low sensory specificity in high-stakes scenes.

### Delivery and Abduction Sequence
- Check sequence is visceral, spatially coherent, and temporally clear.
- Failure pattern: confusion or emotional flattening.

## 4) Character and Dialogue
### Voice Separation
- Check clear voice difference: public persona vs private operative state.
- Failure pattern: identical tone across masks.

### Supporting Cast Agency
- Check allies and antagonists make independent choices with consequences.
- Failure pattern: supporting cast exists only to feed exposition.

### Personal vs Political Link
- Check private maternal arc and revolution arc reinforce each other.
- Failure pattern: symbolic link stated but not dramatized.

## 5) Logic, Consistency, and Accuracy
### Character Continuity
- Check names, roles, and behavior logic stay stable unless explained.

### Timeline Integrity
- Check date/time progression inside chapters and across chapters.
- Require explicit mention of inconsistent timestamps when found.

### Plot Logic and Convenience
- Check evasion, breaches, and rescues avoid coincidence dependence.
- Require explicit alternative if convenience is currently carrying a scene.

## Future-Proof Enhancements
- Evaluate adaptation readiness: audio clarity, visual scene geography.
- Evaluate commercial pull: first-10-pages hook velocity and chapter-end irreversibility.
- Evaluate sequel elasticity: closure now, future runway without cliff-tax.
