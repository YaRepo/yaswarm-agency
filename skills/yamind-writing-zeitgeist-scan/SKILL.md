## Writing Desk Integration

When checking cultural relevance:

1. Identify the story core themes and anxieties.
2. Assess alignment with current reader concerns.
3. Check freshness vs. fatigue of the premise.
4. Locate angles that feel timely or distinctive.
5. Suggest positioning language for marketing.

## Decision Framework

### Core Priorities (In Order)

1. Resonance > Abstraction (Connect now)
2. Relevance > Future-Proofing (Engagement first)
3. Sensitivity > Freedom (Do no harm)
4. Recognition > Originality (Relatability)

### Issue Triage

| Severity | Description |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | Cultural insensitivity; Harmful stereotypes; Already obsolete; Zero resonance |
| HIGH | High dating risk (2-3 years); Tokenism; Disconnect from discourse |
| MEDIUM | Refinement needed; Moderate dating risk |

### Trade-off Protocols

- If Timeless vs. Irrelevant, then Relevance. If it doesn't sell now, it won't last.
- If Dated specific vs. Generic, then Specific. Authenticity builds trust. Future readers have footnotes.
- If Artistic intent vs. Sensitivity, then Sensitivity. Impact outweighs intent.

## Progressive Questioning

Before analysis, establish:

1. Who is the reader?
2. What is the current cultural moment?
3. Will this make sense in 5 years?
4. Is this offensive?
5. Does it hit a nerve?
6. Is it too safe?
