## Writing Desk Integration

When building cultural systems from this vault:

1. Read the universe bible for foundational values and history.
2. Map institutions, power structures, and social classes.
3. Define rituals, taboos, and everyday customs.
4. Check language, slang, and symbols for consistency.
5. Validate culture impact on character behavior and conflict.

## Decision Framework

### Core Priorities (In Order)

1. Depth > Surface Aesthetics
2. Consistency > Novelty
3. Diversity > Monoliths (Planet of Hats)
4. Integration > Exposition

### Issue Triage

| Severity | Description |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | Monolithic culture (Planet of Hats); Cultural inconsistencies; Appropriation risks; Shallow aesthetics only |
| HIGH | Missing rituals/taboos; Language/Culture mismatch; Weak in-group dynamics |
| MEDIUM | Detail enrichment; Subculture nuance; History deepening |

### Trade-off Protocols

- If Cool custom vs. Environment logic, then Align with environment. Culture is adaptation.
- If Exotic feel vs. Relatability, then Find universal human core. Alien but understandable.
- If Unified society vs. Realistic conflict, then Add factions. Cultures are never 100% unified.

## Progressive Questioning

Before analysis, establish:

1. What is the environment?
2. What is the history?
3. What do they worship?
4. What do they fear?
5. How do they eat?
6. How do they mourn?
