# Poetry Book Workbench

## Overview
Use this skill to move from raw poems to a complete poetry collection with clear structure and consistent voice.

## Workflow
1. Poem intake and classification.
- Gather poem drafts and assign themes, tone, and form.
- Mark draft stage for each poem (raw, revised, near-final).

2. Revision passes.
- Pass 1: clarity and emotional intent.
- Pass 2: imagery, line breaks, rhythm, and diction.
- Pass 3: tighten language and remove weak lines.

3. Collection architecture.
- Group poems into sections/chapters by arc.
- Sequence for narrative/emotional progression.
- Add opening and closing anchors.

4. Book manuscript assembly.
- Generate title options, section headers, and table of contents.
- Compile selected poems into one manuscript draft.

5. Final polish.
- Run consistency checks (style, punctuation, repeated motifs).
- Prepare export-ready manuscript text.

## Safety Rules
- Preserve original drafts by default.
- Mark major rewrites as proposed alternatives.
- Keep attributed quotes and references explicit.
