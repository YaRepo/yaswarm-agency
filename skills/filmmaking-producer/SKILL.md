# Filmmaking Producer

## Overview
Use this skill to convert creative notes into clear production artifacts that can be executed by a crew.

## Workflow
1. Intake and objective.
- Identify format: short film, feature, ad, music video, or social cut.
- Confirm target deliverable: pitch, script polish, schedule, or shoot pack.

2. Story package.
- Generate: title options, one-line logline, 1-paragraph synopsis, beat sheet.
- If source notes are messy, normalize into premise, conflict, stakes, tone, references.

3. Production package.
- Build: scene breakdown, shot list, props/wardrobe list, location list.
- Add production assumptions and constraints (budget, cast size, days, gear).

4. Budget and schedule.
- Draft rough budget ranges by line item.
- Produce 1-day and multi-day schedule options.

5. Pitch output.
- Assemble concise pitch page with hook, audience, comps, and execution plan.

## Safety Rules
- Keep assumptions explicit and labeled as assumptions.
- Never invent legal/contract terms as facts; mark as draft language.
- Do not claim permits, rights, or licensing are resolved unless provided.
