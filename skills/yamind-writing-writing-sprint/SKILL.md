# Writing Sprint

Timed writing exercises using your Writing Desk content.

## Instructions

When the user wants a writing sprint:

1. **Set Up Sprint**:
 - Duration (10, 15, 20, 30 minutes)
 - Word goal (optional)
 - Project to work on (or freewrite)

2. **Generate Prompt** based on:
 - Current work's next scene
 - Character development exercise
 - World-building exploration
 - Random creative prompt

3. **Track Results**:
 - Log in daily journal
 - Update work word count
 - Celebrate progress!

---

## Sprint Types

### 📖 Project Sprint
Continue your work-in-progress:
```markdown
**Sprint:** 20 minutes
**Project:** [[📝 Work Title]]
**Goal:** Continue from where you left off

**Context:**
[Summary of last scene/chapter]

**Your mission:**
Write the next scene. Don't edit, just write.
Focus on: [specific element - dialogue, action, emotion]

⏱️ Timer starts now. GO!
```

### 👤 Character Sprint
Deep dive into a character:
```markdown
**Sprint:** 15 minutes
**Character:** [[Character Name]]

**Prompt:** Write a scene from [Character]'s past that explains their [fear/desire/quirk].

OR

**Prompt:** [Character] is alone and thinking about [recent story event]. What's going through their mind?

⏱️ Timer starts now. GO!
```

### 🌍 World-Building Sprint
Expand your universe:
```markdown
**Sprint:** 15 minutes
**Universe:** [[🌌 Universe Name]]

**Prompt:** Describe a typical day for a common person in [Location]. What do they see, hear, worry about?

OR

**Prompt:** Write the legend that every child in this world knows by heart.

⏱️ Timer starts now. GO!
```

### ✨ Random Prompt Sprint
Creative exercise:
```markdown
**Sprint:** 10 minutes

**Random Prompt:**
[Generated prompt from list below]

Rules:
- Use a character from your current project
- Set it in your universe
- Don't overthink, just write!

⏱️ Timer starts now. GO!
```

## Random Prompt Bank

### Dialogue Prompts
- Two characters argue about something trivial that's really about something important
- A confession that changes everything
- Someone lying badly
- Teaching someone something difficult
- A goodbye that isn't final

### Scene Prompts
- The moment just before everything changes
- Someone discovers a secret they wish they hadn't
- A celebration that goes wrong
- Finding something lost
- The calm before the storm

### Character Prompts
- Your character's hands (what do they reveal?)
- The lie they tell themselves every day
- Their most prized possession and why
- A habit they can't break
- What they do when no one's watching

### Sensory Prompts
- Write a scene using only sounds
- Describe a meal that's more than food
- A place that smells like memory
- The texture of loss
- Colors of an emotion

```markdown
## 🏁 Sprint Complete!

**Duration:** [X] minutes
**Words Written:** [X] words
**Words/Minute:** [X]

### Quick Reflection
- What flowed easily?
- What felt stuck?
- Surprise moments?

### Next Steps
- [ ] Keep what you wrote
- [ ] Extract good lines for later
- [ ] Continue this thread tomorrow
- [ ] Discard (that's okay too!)

---
*Logged to [[Journal - YYYY-MM-DD]]*
```

## Sprint Challenges

### Word Wars
Set a specific word count goal:
- **Easy:** 250 words in 15 min (17 wpm)
- **Medium:** 500 words in 20 min (25 wpm)
- **Hard:** 1,000 words in 30 min (33 wpm)
- **Insane:** 1,667 words in 60 min (NaNoWriMo pace)

### Streak Challenge
Write sprints on consecutive days:
- 3 days = Warming up
- 7 days = Building momentum
- 14 days = Forming a habit
- 30 days = You're unstoppable

## Example Usage

User: "I need a writing sprint"

Response:
1. Ask: Project sprint or creative exercise?
2. If project: Read current work, find stopping point
3. Generate contextual prompt
4. Set timer and word goal
5. After completion: Log results, celebrate

User: "Give me a random prompt for 15 minutes"

Response:
1. Pick random prompt from bank
2. Customize with their universe/characters
3. Set 15-minute goal
4. Encourage: No editing, just flow!

## Safety Rules
- Prefer reversible, minimal changes first.
- Do not overwrite user content without explicit confirmation.
- Report assumptions and blockers instead of guessing hidden requirements.

## Output Contract
1. Clear execution summary for what was done and why.

3. Validation status and remaining risks.
