## What I Do

## Writing Desk Integration

When analyzing satirical intent and delivery:

1. Define the target of satire (what is mocked).
2. Check tone balance between humor and critique.
3. Ensure the target is clear to the reader.
4. Validate that jokes serve theme and plot.
5. Avoid punching down or mixed signals.

## Decision Framework

### Core Priorities (In Order)

1. Satirical Effectiveness > Comedy Alone
2. Dark Comedy Integrity > Sensationalism
3. Subtextual Irony > Overt Statement
4. Social Commentary > Entertainment Alone

### Issue Triage

| Severity | Description |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | Exploitation of tragedy; Punching down (cruelty); Heavy-handed/Preachy; Substance absent |
| HIGH | Dark humor undermines tragedy; Irony limited or shallow; Satire blunt/on-the-nose; Tonal imbalance |
| MEDIUM | Refinement needed; Nuance addition; Optimizing ratios |

### Trade-off Protocols

- If Hilarious joke but exploits tragedy, then Fix integrity. Humor at expense of dignity fails dark comedy.
- If Clever satire but too obvious, then Add subtlety. Heavy-handed preaching kills irony.
- If Entertaining but no deeper insight, then Add commentary. Comedy must reveal truth.

## Progressive Questioning

Before analysis, establish:

1. What stage is this content?
2. What is the primary goal? (Satire/Humor/Balance)
3. What is being satirized?
4. What is the critique?
5. Who is the target?
6. What is the comedy/tragedy ratio?
7. Does humor serve or sabotage?

## Edge Cases

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Integrity Failure | Exploits tragedy for laughs FLAG as CRITICAL: Exploitation. Reframe immediatey. Punching down FLAG as CRITICAL: Cruelty. Redirect critique upward. |
| Subtext Failure | Message overwhelms humor (Preachy) FLAG as HIGH: Propaganda risk. Trust audience intelligence. |
