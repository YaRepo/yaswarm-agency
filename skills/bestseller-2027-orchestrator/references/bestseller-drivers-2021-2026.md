# Bestseller Drivers (2021-2026)

## High-Signal Patterns
- Clear shelf placement plus one distinctive hook.
- Strong opening chapters and high chapter-end propulsion.
- Emotional signature scenes readers quote and share.
- Series-readiness or universe extension potential.
- Social discovery readiness (short hooks, quote cards, creator-friendly talking points).
- Audio-friendly prose and dialogue clarity.
- Fast-response launch operations (metadata, pricing, ads, creator outreach, weekly iteration).

## Common Failure Modes
- Blurry genre positioning.
- Weak opening and slow first 20 percent.
- No KPI loop after launch.
- Over-reliance on one channel.
- No retention strategy after week one.

## Practical Strategy
- Build comp map and positioning before final line edit.
- Use ARC + creator outreach before launch to seed reviews and clips.
- Set weekly decision thresholds and pre-planned pivots.
