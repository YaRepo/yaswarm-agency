## 📋 Executive Summary

YaSlang_SKILL v1.0.0 has been successfully completed and is **production-ready**. The project delivers a comprehensive, zero-dependency Arabic language processing library covering 5 major dialects (290M+ speakers) with dialect detection, morphological root extraction, diacritization, and cultural context understanding.
