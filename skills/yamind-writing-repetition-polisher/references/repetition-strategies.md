# Repetition Strategies

Use these techniques to reduce repetition while keeping the voice rich and alive.

## 1) Replace with Action Beats (Prefer Over Dialogue Tags)

Instead of repeating tags or a single verb, show movement or intention.

Example:
- "I don't care," she said. "I don't care," she said.
Rewrite:
- "I don't care." She crushed the badge between her fingers.
- "I don't care." He checked the door latch, twice.

## 2) Shift Syntax, Not Just Words

Change the sentence structure to refresh rhythm.

Example:
- He opened the door. He opened the window.
Rewrite:
- The door opened first; the window followed.

## 3) Swap Detail Focus

Keep the action but vary the concrete detail.

Example:
- The street was dark. The alley was dark.
Rewrite:
- The street was dark; the alley smelled of wet metal.

## 4) Compress or Combine

Merge repeated ideas into a single stronger line.

Example:
- He was tired. He was exhausted.
Rewrite:
- He was tired in the way that makes you feel hollow.

## 5) Use Pronouns and Implication

If the subject is clear, avoid re-naming it.

Example:
- Marcus stepped in. Marcus sat.
Rewrite:
- Marcus stepped in, then sat.

## 6) Change the Verb Class

Replace a repeated verb with a different action class.

Example:
- She looked at the screen. She looked at the floor.
Rewrite:
- She scanned the screen, then dropped her gaze to the floor.

## 7) Rotate the Sensory Channel

Move from visual to sound, texture, or internal sensation.

Example:
- The lights were bright. The corridor was bright.
Rewrite:
- The lights buzzed; the corridor tasted of hot dust.

## 8) Intentional Echo (Use Sparingly)

Repetition can work when it is a deliberate emphasis.

Rules:
- Use for mantra, dread, or mounting pressure.
- Keep to a short run (2 to 3 uses max).
- Make it obvious that the echo is intentional.

## 9) Global vs Local Repetition

- Local repeats (same paragraph) are more distracting.
- Global repeats (across chapters) can become motif, but only if varied.

## 10) Checklist

- Does a repeated word add emphasis or just noise?
- Can an action beat replace a tag?
- Can a sentence be merged?
- Can the detail shift to a new sensory channel?
