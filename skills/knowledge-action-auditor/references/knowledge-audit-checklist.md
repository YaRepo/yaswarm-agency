# Knowledge Audit Checklist

Use this checklist during manual review after scanner triage.

## 1. Character Knowledge Consistency

- Who knows what, and from which scene?
- Does any character act on information before obtaining it?
- Does any character forget previously learned facts without cause?
- Is every major knowledge jump traceable to evidence?

## 2. Shapeshifter Behavior Plausibility

- Are there suspicious behaviors others should notice?
- If others do not notice, is there a believable distraction/redirection?
- Do post-reveal reactions match each character's established psychology?

## 3. Clues and Red Herrings

- Are clues present early enough to be fair?
- Are red herrings motivated by character/world logic (not author convenience)?
- Does foreshadowing reward attentive readers without making cast oblivious?

## 4. World Rules and Inference Limits

- Are shapeshifter constraints explicit and consistently followed?
- If rules are broken, is the exception justified in-story?
- Do any characters have expertise that should accelerate discovery?

## 5. Convenience and Motivation

- Why is the secret revealed to this person and at this moment?
- Why is secrecy maintained from others despite mounting pressure?
- Are non-discovery outcomes driven by real stakes, not plot shielding?

## 6. Reader Clarity

- Can a reader track who currently knows the truth?
- Are POV and internal monologue boundaries clear?
- Are reveal transitions legible and emotionally earned?
