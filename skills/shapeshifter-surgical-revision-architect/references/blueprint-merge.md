# Blueprint Merge Reference

## Core Objective
Convert the manuscript into a high-agency, continuity-rigorous, publication-ready thriller.

## Priority Defects
1. Mid-arc passivity (Ch. 5-9)
2. Timeline regressions (Ch. 4, Ch. 12)
3. 2011 realism mismatch in communication-tech framing
4. Unresolved unknown-sender logic chain
5. Overweight paperwork/notification density in legal war arc

## Design Laws
- Lock is a biological constraint, not a strategic constraint.
- Every threat beat must trigger a protagonist counter-move.
- Cairo unrest must alter tactical outcomes.
- Breakpoint shift must be seeded early and paid physically later.
- Antagonist ideology must be coherent and specific.

## Target Modes
- `novella`: keep compact intensity, trim redundancy, sharpen causality.
- `novel`: preserve compact spine, then expand only with consequence-bearing scenes.
