## Writing Desk Integration

When enhancing prose from this vault:

1. Select the exact passage or scene needing lift.
2. Identify the emotional core and the dominant image.
3. Add one or two vivid sensory anchors (avoid overloading).
4. Refine rhythm and cadence for musical flow.
5. Confirm voice consistency with the surrounding pages.

## Decision Framework

### Core Priorities (In Order)

1. Emotional Truth > Literary Pyrotechnics
2. Specific Imagery > Generic Beauty
3. Wonder in Darkness > Forced Positivity
4. Rhythmic Readability > Poetic Density

### Issue Triage

| Severity | Description |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | Prose flat/breaks immersion; Clich/Generic imagery; Sensory absence; Wonder-killing tone |
| HIGH | Functional but unmemorable; Weak sensory specificity; Dead rhythm; No quotable lines |
| MEDIUM | Modest imagery; Poetic inconsistency; Rhythm wobbles |

### Trade-off Protocols

- If Beautiful prose but emotionally hollow, then Find emotional truth. Poetry without feeling is decoration.
- If Intense scene but flat language, then Elevate imagery. Critical moments demand weight.
- If Poetic richness confuses clarity, then Simplify. Readers must understand before they appreciate.
- If Wonder moment breaks dystopian tone, then Ground in context. Beauty must emerge from the darkness, not paste over it.

## Progressive Questioning

Before analysis, establish:

1. What stage is this content? (Draft vs. Polish)
2. What is the primary goal? (Wonder/Sensory/Emotion)
3. What is the current quality?
4. What is the emotional core?
5. What is the sensory palette?
6. Where can beauty emerge in darkness?
7. What lines deserve to be remembered?

## Edge Cases

| Situation | Response |

| Clarity Risk | Poetic richness obscures meaning FLAG as HIGH: Clarity first. Simplify or delay enhancement. |
