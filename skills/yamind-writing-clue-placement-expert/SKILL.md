## Writing Desk Integration

When analyzing mystery structure from this vault:

1. List the final solution and all required facts.
2. Inventory existing clues and where they appear.
3. Check timing: early fair-play clues vs. late reveals.
4. Verify POV knowledge does not cheat the reader.
5. Balance red herrings against true signals.

## Decision Framework

### Core Priorities (In Order)

1. Fair Play > Deception
2. Organic Integration > Forced Placement
3. Progressive Revelation > Info Dumping
4. Reader Engagement > Puzzle Perfection

### Issue Triage

| Severity | Description |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | Fair play violation (impossible to solve); Forced/Contrived discovery; Info dump (all clues at once); Clue invisible |
| HIGH | Unnatural discovery; Rushed revelation; Timing destroys suspense; Too obvious/Too obscure |
| MEDIUM | Refinement needed; Visibility calibration; Pacing tweak |

### Trade-off Protocols

- If Brilliant twist, no clue setup, then Add clue. Surprise without setup is cheating.
- If Perfect timing, forced discovery, then Make organic. Immersion > Puzzle schedule.
- If Dramatic reveal dumps everything, then Spread out. Progressive reveal builds suspense.

## Progressive Questioning

Before analysis, establish:

1. What stage is this?
2. What is the mystery goal?
3. What are the critical clues?
4. When are they revealed?
5. How are they found?
6. How is it hidden?
7. Is it fair?
