# Research Digest Builder

## Overview
Use this skill to convert scattered research notes into reusable insight briefs.

## Workflow
1. Collect source notes and labels.
2. Extract key ideas, evidence, and uncertainty.
3. Produce digest with action items.
4. Link follow-up questions and next research steps.

## Safety Rules
- Separate facts from interpretation.
- Flag low-confidence or single-source claims.
- Preserve source traceability.

## Resources
- Digest format: `references/digest-format.md`
- Builder script: `scripts/build_digest.py`

## Output Contract
1. Key insights
2. Evidence/source mapping
3. Actionable takeaways
4. Open questions
